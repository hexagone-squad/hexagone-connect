# IT Architecture Review Agenda (POC)

Status: TRAINING / POC / NOT FOR PRODUCTION
Owner: Technology, Product & Architecture Lead

## Inputs

- [Decision readiness register](decision-readiness-register.md)
- [Tenant-boundary scenario matrix](tenant-boundary-scenario-matrix.md)
- [Engineering readiness and POC contribution guide](engineering-readiness-poc-guide.md)

## Agenda

1. Confirm baseline evidence reuse
- Validate each open decision references existing ADRs, governance checks, or service documentation.

2. Review unresolved decisions
- Walk through open entries in the decision register.
- For each entry: confirm missing business input owner and due date.

3. Review tenant-boundary risks
- Review deny/allow outcomes in the scenario matrix.
- Flag scenarios requiring policy or architecture decisions.

4. Assign next POCs and evidence owners
- Confirm required POC owner, scope boundary, and validation evidence.

5. Decide ADR promotion candidates
- Identify entries ready for ADR draft or constitution/governance amendment proposals.

## Expected outputs

- Updated decision register statuses and review dates.
- Named owners for each missing business input.
- Ranked list of next architecture questions and evidence gaps.
